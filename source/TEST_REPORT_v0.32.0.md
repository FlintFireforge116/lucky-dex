# LuckyDex v0.32.0 — Regional Adventure + Ball Drop release

## Delivered systems
- Three populated compressed campaigns: **Kanto (Red/Blue), Johto (Gold/Silver), Hoenn (Emerald)**. Each has eight appropriate named Gym Leaders, four Elite Four and its Champion, with canonical teams (including repeated species), 27 distinct route/location pools and environmental events across the three regions. Fixed Blue Blastoise variant and Tate & Liza sequential singles are documented adaptations.
- Nine-region registry. Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea are visibly marked unpopulated. Engine configuration supports variable checkpoint counts, trial checkpoints and tournament finale sequences; it does not assume every future region has eight Gyms and four Elite Four.
- Seeded regional run reducer stores branching choices, party HP/status/PP, battle RNG, opposing team position, individual claims and completion. The phone map shows the current chapter, available encounter choices, next Gym, badge progress, total region progress and battle supplies. Wild/trainer, camp/TM/event/shop, rare/cache choices have materially different rewards and recovery.
- Pokemon levels/EXP retain the persistent ownership model and existing curve `80 + 30 × level`, cap 50. Levels affect combat. Regional battle levels start capped at 8 and grow with chapter caps; permanent levels are preserved. Battle EXP is awarded for every defeated foe and also advances owned partners. New level-up moves either fill free slots or create explicit replacement offers.
- **1,025 species / 693 real moves / 14,929 level-up entries / 30 curated TMs / 7,849 verified TM compatibility entries**, generated from pinned PokeAPI data. Actual basic moves favour appropriate weak moves; 47 stronger exceptions and 14 status starters are documented. No type-wide fake Quick Hit is presented as the Pokémon’s move. PP, types, accuracy, power and simplified roles affect the battle reducer.
- TMs persist in a separate case, with search, compatible Pokémon selection, four-move replacement and consumption only after confirmation. Owned and regional sets may differ; high-level permanent learning cannot bypass a campaign cap, and regional replacement is explicit.
- Ball Drop now renders and collides with the same mirrored sloped triangles/wall ramps. Aiming, reward slots, neon presentation, all power-pin effects and normal gravity remain. Secondary recovery uses only a tiny deterministic impulse, without position teleporting.

## Save/update compatibility
- Storage key remains `luckyDexSave`, saveVersion 7. `adventureRegional` schema 1 and `tmInventory` are additive. Valid owned levels, EXP, counts, keys, firstSeen and unknown metadata are retained; missing level defaults to 5. Cards, currency, existing progression, Dash, packs and minigame records are preserved.
- Legacy Roam/expeditions remain reachable and existing active expeditions resume. Legacy preference and a Regional Campaigns return entry preserve navigation. Their battle/move system also uses the shared real-move progression framework.
- Actual Chrome migration tests retain old Pokémon counts, currencies, cards and legacy fields; battle reload/background preserves exact committed HP and RNG. Capture does not duplicate a run party member or create a fourth saved team slot. Collection milestones and existing Trainer XP/daily hooks remain integrated.
- Version **0.32.0 / 320**, npm/lockfile, runtime build marker and visible text match. Package remains `com.example.luckydex`. The included development keystore is byte-identical to v0.31.0 and the APK certificate remains `069b440b7d315efba1f15473109ae3d945ad1fa8059e6aa262726c986d398a6a`.
- Changed existing runtime files are limited to Adventure battle/controller, shared app migration/EXP integration, Ball Drop and index script/style loading. Unrelated booster JSON regenerated by the established audit was semantically identical and restored byte-for-byte. Achievement expansion, pack theatrics, collection sorting, Voltorb exploits and prize currencies were not changed.
