LuckyDex v0.69.130 — Pokémon rarity challenges

- Added a featured Pokémon rarity focus in Classic TCG Auto Build: Any / Common / Uncommon / Rare-Holo / Ultra Rare.
- Added a featured Pokémon gameplay diamond rarity focus in TCG Pocket Auto Build (1◇ to 4◇), independent of the existing owned-print maximum-rarity cap.
- Added Basic Pokémon only options to both builders for unevolved challenge decks.
- Preserved legal evolution prerequisites and Trainer support even when those cards are lower rarity.
- Added manual catalogue rarity filters to Classic and Pocket deck editors.
- Added CPU opponent rarity / Basic-only preferences in Classic random battles and Pocket Random Solo battles.
- Kept Pocket fixed official Solo decks and their difficulty levels unchanged.
- Corrected Pocket diamond rarity parser to accept the printed ◊ character used by card data.
- Does not change card ownership, card text, pack rarity or battle rewards.
