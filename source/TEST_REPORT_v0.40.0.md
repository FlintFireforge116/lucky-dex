# LuckyDex v0.40.0 — Adventure 3.0

Authoritative input: supplied `LuckyDex_source_v0.39.1.zip`. Release: **0.40.0 / Android versionCode 4000**, **com.example.luckydex**, original signer retained.

## Adventure changes

Adventure now has one public controller and one home screen. Continue Journey, Wild Hunt, Party, Poké Mart, Bag, TM Case/Moves and Field Guide share its navigation. The old Legacy selector, branching Expedition, run perks, guardian progression, retreat framing and Collection-owned Party editor are removed from active gameplay. Collection keeps its read-only team filter and links Pokémon inspection into Adventure.

Kanto, Johto and Hoenn each keep an independent persistent journey. Named authored locations replace anonymous branching nodes: four or five sections per Gym chapter, a three-section Victory Road chapter, then deliberate Elite Four and Champion preparation/battles. Optional supply stops, rare sightings, Wild Hunts and town merchants enrich local travel. Badges and safe city checkpoints are permanent. Defeat heals at the latest checkpoint and restarts that chapter; League defeat restarts the League at its Center. Leaving or reloading preserves location, party HP/PP, encounter RNG and battle state. Completed journeys remain inspectable and replayable without resetting completion records or one-time claims. Later regions remain unavailable.

The existing scene families, weather, hotspot effects, Pokémon actors and battle backdrops are reused locally. Gym preparation clearly identifies leader/city/type and stages the leader’s actual featured Pokémon rather than mislabelling a generic trainer portrait. Party and move menus use readable paper panels and pixel sprites. Screens own a scrollable content pane with reachable controls, and both system and in-app reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Wild Hunt preserves habitat records, hotspot discovery and catch-chain math: rare bonus is 0.2 percentage points per catch up to +2%; item discovery bonus is 0.3 points per catch up to +3%. Capture extends the chain; victory, loss, fleeing or leaving a revealed Pokémon without capture breaks it. Navigation without abandoning an encounter does not break the chain. UI shows the chain, actual bonuses and useful milestones. The old legendary regression fixture incorrectly requested a legendary in the meadow, whose pool has none; its replacement tests the real coastal legendary pool without changing the 1/400 rule.

Party owns add/remove/replace/reorder/lead operations, three-member limits, Summary/Moves/Stats and current HP/status. **Collection Lv. 30 · Journey Lv. 8** wording makes level syncing explicit. Injuries and resting-member PP/status survive removal/re-add and region switching. Party editing and teaching are locked during active battles.

One shared move view presents name, capitalized type, Physical/Special/Status, power, accuracy, PP and factual supported effects. Unsupported effects are explicitly labelled. TM teaching and pending moves compare all four replacement slots before confirmation. Cancel, stale selections, imported-save replacement, failed save or rapid taps do not consume TMs; successful teaching consumes exactly once and updates the appropriate journey/reserve slots.

Poké Mart retains its existing economy, including three Potions for 180 coins; merchants offer the existing two for 100. Balances, Trainer Rank, owned quantities, lock reasons and shortfalls are visible. Confirmation previews both currency and item counts, then revalidates and commits debit/grant together. Tokens block duplicate taps; failed persistence rolls back. Bag explains actual supplies and enables field healing only for an injured living journey partner.

## Migration and reward safety

`adventureRegional` becomes schema 2 with per-region journeys and an active-region alias rebound after serialization. An active old regional journey maps earned badges to its next safe checkpoint, preserving permanent Pokémon levels/EXP, catches, currency, inventory, TMs and unrelated records. Old active state is retained as historical input. Historical completed records alone do not skip the new campaign. An already-paid active regional clear keeps its reward flag and migrated claim flags, preventing another clear payment; completing its migrated remaining travel still produces a replayable completion record.

Old active Expedition state is archived and cleared without granting an unclaimed reward. Wild Hunt chains, seen/caught/rare/shiny records and existing committed catches survive. Migration is idempotent. Claim flags and grants share the synchronous save path; defeat, refresh, replay and back-navigation cannot repeat one-time supplies, badges or final rewards. An all-fainted selected party is recovered rather than left in a terminal-battle lock.

## Battle and balance

FIGHT opens the move menu, with BAG and POKÉMON at the command level. Trainer capture/berries/leave are rejected; ordinary and rare wild encounters retain LEAVE. Opponent team counts, status names, misses, effectiveness, fainting and unsupported moves have readable feedback. Real moves resolve **priority → Speed → seeded ties**. A fainted queued attacker or replacement cannot inherit a queued attack, and an action prevented by sleep/paralysis does not consume PP. Normal resistance against Rock/Steel and status-only enemy healing were corrected.

Canonical priority is regenerated from the included pinned CSV. Existing adapted combat remains intentional: 1.1× STAB, 95-power combat ceiling/40-equivalent variable moves, simplified attack/defence stance, accessible 0.60 regional incoming-damage factor, and simple item/switch/capture ordering. This is not a competitive cartridge simulator. Details are in `docs/ADVENTURE_BATTLE_V040.md`.

The updated real-battle simulator completed **12/12 journeys**: three low-level seeds in each region plus one high-collection-level case per region. It uses actual battle reducers, earned TMs/caches and transactional Mart purchases, with no injected wins or free supplies. Main paths contain 31 battles; one League recovery raised a case to 34. Results: 228–450 combat actions, 21–54 Potions, 1–7 Mart bundles, ending balances 5,068–6,248 coins and eight Gym TMs. Starting level-50 collections synced to eight; low-level parties finished at journey level 29–30. This describes prepared parties with type coverage, not an untouched starter guarantee. Authored Centers now fully recover HP/PP/status; reward/cost and combat coefficients were otherwise retained. See `docs/ADVENTURE_BALANCE_V040.md` and `artifacts/v0400/regional-balance.json`.
