EX Sandstorm — September 18, 2003
- Adds all 100 cards: 92 Pokémon, 7 Trainer cards (including three Fossils), and 1 Special Energy.
- Card artwork, names, numbering, HP, attack costs, evolution data, Weakness/Resistance, retreat, rarity, and card strategy metadata.
- 154 attacks, 28 printed Poké-Powers/Poké-Bodies (including Fossil Bodies), attack descriptions, and seven Pokémon-ex with two-Prize rules.
- EX Sandstorm registered for Classic battle, deck-building, Test Lab unrestricted selection, owned-card mapping, and opponent deck expansion filters.
- CPU restricted to EX Sandstorm can build a legal 30-card Classic hybrid deck.
- Implements numerous unique attacks, damage scaling, healing, status effects, attack locks, random rolls, Energy rules and active/bench interactions.
- Sandstorm passives implemented for Armaldo Supporter restrictions, Cradily retreat prevention, Cacturne/Cacnea Poison Payback, Arcanine/Growlithe Fire Veil, Ludicolo/Lombre/Lotad/Root Fossil Rain Dish, Volbeat's retreat discount, Illumise/Arbok/Kabuto damage reduction, Kecleon's types, Aerodactyl ex Primal Lock, Wobbuffet's Safeguard, Zangoose's Poison Resistance and Claw Fossil Jagged Stone.
- Multi Energy provides one flexible Energy when alone, or one Colorless if another Special Energy is attached; it does not generate eight Energy.
- Sand Swirl verified to deal 20 actual damage AND apply its retreat lock.
- Preserves 30-card hybrid Classic rules and Energy Zone, and the previous Test Lab and Skyridge fixes.

Validation:
- 11 EX Sandstorm test groups, with all 154 attacks exercised through the battle engine on two seeded random paths.
- Additional cross-expansion regression tests for .158–.162 passed.
- No Android on-device/touchscreen test in this environment; interactions outside automated scenarios still merit playtesting.
