LuckyDex v0.69.159 — Gen 3 damage-counter attack correction

Base version: v0.69.158. Apply this patch to the unpacked source/ tree.

FIXED
- EX Ruby & Sapphire Vigoroth (ex1-47) Rage now adds 10 damage per counter (rather than 100).
  The battle engine stores accumulated damage in HP units (10 per counter), so its effect
  multiplier must be 1. With 10 damage taken, Rage correctly previews and deals 30 (not 120).
- EX Ruby & Sapphire Goldeen (ex1-55) Flail had the identical data error. Its damage now
  correctly equals the damage Goldeen has taken (10 per counter).
- Corrected both previews in the active Pokémon move panel and executed battle damage.

QUALITY CONTROL
- Added regression coverage for Vigoroth Rage at 0–60 existing damage and Goldeen Flail
  at 0–40 existing damage, verifying preview, actual attack log and defender damage.
- Re-ran EX Ruby & Sapphire battle / card audit and release metadata tests.
- No changes to existing energy zone, prizes, game format, opponent deck choice or other sets.
