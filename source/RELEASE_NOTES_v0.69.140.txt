LuckyDex v0.69.140 — Unown word-Power hand + field adaptation
Version code 7120. Apply after v0.69.139.

Classic TCG's 3-Bench rule makes four-letter Unown word Powers impossible if
all letters must be Benched at once. For the following word Power requirements,
recognise a required Unown letter if it is present in your hand, Active Spot,
or Bench. The Pokémon whose Power is used MUST be in play as normal; copies
held solely in hand cannot activate Powers. Each different letter is required;
Unown from an opponent's hand/field never counts.

Neo Destiny:
  Unown [G] [Give] — G I V E; on heads attach the next ordered Basic Energy
    from your Energy Zone to a chosen Pokémon (Energy Zone adaptation).
  Unown [H] [Help] — H E L P; shuffle hand into deck and redraw the same count.
  Unown [W] [Want] — W A N T; on heads choose Trainer from discard into hand.
  These three previously disabled cards are now battle-enabled: 113/113.

Neo Discovery:
  Unown [F] [Find] — F I N D; search your deck for a Trainer card.
  Unown [U] [Undo] — U N D O; return your Active Pokémon to hand.
  Both support the same mixed hand/in-play letter requirements.

Other Unown Powers without four-letter requirements are unchanged. This is a
specific house-rule adaptation to a 3-Bench game, not a printed-card erratum.
No changes to card ownership, Booster packs, simulation settings or rewards.

Tests: 62 focused regression tests passed, including 9 new targeted Unown tests.
Existing unrelated Neo Discovery Metal-only CPU recipe test still fails on
v0.69.139 baseline and is not part of this patch.
