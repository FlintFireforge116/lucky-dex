HOW TO PLAY

TCG Pocket Battle

Goal

Score 3 points before the CPU. Knocking Out a normal Pokémon scores 1 point; higher-value cards will score their Pocket value as support expands.

Controls

Actions now live where they happen: use attacks and retreat on your Active Pokémon, play cards from your hand, drag Energy from the bottom-right Energy Zone onto a Pokémon, and tap either discard pile to inspect it.

Important rules

Decks contain 20 cards with at most 2 copies of a gameplay card. Your Bench holds up to 3 Pokémon. The first player gets no Energy on turn 1. Weakness adds 20 damage. Solo battles end at turn 50.

Current slice

Opening Active/Bench setup is automatic for now. Battle-enabled cards are expanding from Genetic Apex as the reusable effect engine grows; unsupported cards remain collectible and are never simulated incorrectly.

Tip

Build a backup attacker before your Active Pokémon is knocked out. Drag Energy onto the Pokémon you want to prepare, and remember you can inspect either discard pile at any time.
