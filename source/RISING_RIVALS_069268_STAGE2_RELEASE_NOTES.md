# Rising Rivals v0.69.268 – Stage 2 continuation

- Starts from v0.69.267 with 49 playable prints.
- All 120 authentic collector prints remain in the catalogue.
- 89 prints now battle/deck enabled; 31 complex prints stay gated until their effects work.
- Added verified combat and option handlers for 40 additional prints, including Gym Leader Pokémon SP, Lv.X effects, basic search, heal and recovery.
- SP Energy and Upper Energy count as proper single or conditional double Energy units rather than multiple simultaneous types.
- New: Nidoking Territoriality / Giga Horn / Fling Away; Vespiquen Royal Gain / Leaf Needle; Beedrill Needle Shock; Alakazam E4 LV.X Damage Switch; Mismagius GL LV.X Darkness Magic / Magical Return; Snorlax LV.X Exercise and Big Appetite; Bebe's Search; Flint's Willpower; Kecleon Colorful Body.
- Bebe's Search refuses to play when it is the only card in hand.
- Continuous Royal Gain fires only when Grass Energy is attached from hand, not from the basic Energy Zone.
- The card generator uses the bundled authentic print data and refuses unverified effects.
- CI APK build checks both stage 1 and stage 2 Rising Rivals tests.
- 31 remaining cards are *not* described as fully implemented. Arcanine deliberately remains gated because Burn Out's optional damage must be chosen before Weakness and Resistance.
