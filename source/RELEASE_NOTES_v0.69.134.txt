LuckyDex v0.69.134 — Scarlet & Violet Tools and reactive-attack fixes
Requires prior v0.69.133.

FIXES
- Scarlet & Violet Base #169 Defiance Band, #174 Exp. Share, #192 Rock Chestplate,
  #193 Rocky Helmet and #197 Vitality Band now use the internal Tool classifier,
  not the display label "Pokémon Tool". They attach to a Pokémon and are not
  accidentally played/discarded as one-shot Trainers.
- Defiance Band's +30 is applied only while its owner has MORE Prize cards remaining
  than the opposing player (equal Prizes => zero bonus); placing the Tool on turn 1
  is allowed, but it is inactive until its condition is met.
- Attack-damage reactions receive the actual attack-side source metadata, including
  when a repeated attack deals damage. This restores Rocky Helmet retaliation and
  Cacnea/Cacturne Counterattack Quills.
- Counterattack Quills is Active-only, per printed rules.
- Arven can find the corrected Tool cards, and Exp. Share can be played normally.

QA
- 18 newly added targeted audit regressions covering Tool placement, Prize
  comparisons, damage, removals, active-only reactions, knockouts, Arven, and
  all 256 battle-ready SV1 print schema definitions.
- 115 selected Classic/Founja/SV1 regression tests pass.
- Mobile Android gesture/input and exhaustive cross-card rules fidelity are not
  independently proven by these automated checks.
