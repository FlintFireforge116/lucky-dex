LuckyDex v0.69.135 — Deck strategy / metadata foundation
Builds on v0.69.134.
- Shared role and status metadata inferred from actual card effects and printed text, with targeted rule-hook overrides.
- Strategy selections: Balanced, Sleep, Poison, Burn, Paralysis, Healing/stall, Energy acceleration, Disruption.
- Works in Classic and Pocket Auto Build and generated Random CPU decks (official fixed Pocket Solo remains unchanged).
- Type/stage relevance gates prevent off-type Stadiums and Tools from being indiscriminately added to decks.
- Sleep enabler / payoff distinction; self-inflicted Sleep does not count as an opponent status engine.
- Manual card filters for mechanics and strategy on both formats.
- Build-plan report naming enablers, payoffs, consistency support, and weak or missing synergy.
- Preserves legality, rarity focus, Basic-only, existing battle rules, cards, collection and packs.
Known limitations:
- Metadata heuristics require continuing validation for rare card interactions.
- Pockets and Classic remain separate card formats; one combined deck cannot currently mix both.
- This is a rule-derived heuristic planner, not an exhaustive competitive deck search/simulator.
