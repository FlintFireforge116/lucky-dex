- Expansion: Plasma Blast (BW10), following Plasma Freeze (BW9).
- Source: the original English 105-print `PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data` `cards/en/bw10.json` card file, pinned to Git blob `ccddde115a17152e330d29736a3f1665e006d67f`.

Subsequent runs use the cached source.

## Explicit battle effects and mechanics

- BW10 has 105 distinct collector prints and never deduplicates alternate Pokémon / Trainer art variants; each print preserves ID, physical UID, printed attacks, attack costs, HP, stages, types, Abilities and Trainer text.
- BW8/BW9/BW10 Plasma Energy is a distinct **physical Special Energy**; it provides Colorless and cannot be replaced by fresh Basic Energy counters or a Basic Energy Zone. BW10-specific effects can locate all three identical-name prints.
- Solar Transporter inspects the top five cards and physically moves only Team Plasma cards to hand; non-Plasma cards are discarded. Reverse Edge recovers the selected physical card from discard after heads. Fast Forward mills one card per attached Plasma Energy. Fossil Hunt selects exact Item cards bearing `Fossil` in their names.
- Energy Retrieval may select actual Basic Energy in physical discard or Basic Energy in the spent/discard ledger, not from the unused Basic Energy Zone. Emerald Slash searches physical Grass Basic Energy from deck and can use the correct Grass Energy source where the Classic custom Energy Zone permits it. The attacker selects the destination Bench Pokémon for each attachment.
- Master Ball and Strong Bond search a physical deck and preserve UID. Caitlin chooses exact hand cards, placing them on the deck bottom and drawing the same number; no automatic hand replacement. Pokémon Catcher chooses the opponent's Benched Pokémon.
- Gaia Crush sends Stadium to its actual owner's discard. Silver Bangle adds damage only when a non-EX attacker hits an EX Active. Silver Mirror prevents eligible Plasma Pokémon attack effects on non-EX Tool holders, including Benched targets. Iris adds pre-Weakness Active damage for each Prize card opponent has taken.
- G Booster and G Scope are **persistent ACE SPEC Pokémon Tools**, not turn-expiring Technical Machines. Only Genesect-EX holding them may gain their printed attacks; G Booster offers explicit exact-source discard of two Energy, G Scope targets one opponent Bench. The general one-ACE-SPEC-per-deck rule still applies.
- Escavalier Slashing Strike and Ursaring Adrenalash/secret Virizion Leaf Wallop respect next-own-turn restrictions and damage timing. Standard attacks and non-EX safeguards reuse older mechanics with the physical cards preserved.
- CPU deck construction includes BW10 alongside BW8/BW9 and ensures Plasma Energy-dependent attackers obtain real Plasma Energy prints. Stage line prerequisites and Tools' Pokémon partner hints remain visible in generated metadata.

## Deliberately gated mechanics

The source generator marks printed cards as `battleEnabled=false` and `deckEligible=false` when the original printed effect still needs an executable path. In particular: Restored Pokémon discard-pile Abilities; special fossil evolution cases; Red Signal hand attachment trigger; Verdant Wind, Tool Reversal, Toolbox; individual multi-source Energy or counter distribution; Jirachi-EX on-play search; Plasma Transfer; Reversal Trigger; Sinister Hand; and several continuous Ability effects. See `scripts/bw10_verified.py` and `docs/BW10_BUILD_COVERAGE.json` (generated by preparation) for the exact collector-numbered list, including any additional unmatched printed attacks. **Do not claim all 105 prints are battle-enabled.** Never quietly replace complex printed effects with a no-op.
