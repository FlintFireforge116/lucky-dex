# v0.69.558 — Magma Spurt Classic Energy Zone adaptation

## Approved rule
EX4-10 Team Magma's Houndoom, Magma Spurt: roll five coins using the shared attack RNG. For each heads, take the next existing Basic Energy unit from the ordered Energy Zone (if any) and record it in the Basic Energy discard ledger. Fire/Fighting units contribute 30 damage each. Other units are also discarded, without damage. Tails never consume Energy. Empty supply does not manufacture Energy and does not suppress the remaining flips. The current turn's separately drawn Energy, attachments, physical draw deck and physical discard are unchanged. Damage is resolved before the normal matchup/defense pipeline and there is no second post-damage discard. Preserve printed catalogue text; display an explicit hybrid rule in battle. AI estimates expected damage from declared Energy types and finite supply size without inspecting hidden future order or spending RNG. Preview is a non-leaking upper bound (0–150 with at least five units), not a promised result.
