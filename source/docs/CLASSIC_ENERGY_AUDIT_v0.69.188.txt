APPROVED ENERGY ADAPTATION
--------------------------
• Normal Classic Basic Energy originates in an ordered separate Energy Zone.
• Generic effects explicitly SEARCHING the deck for NEW Basic Energy can take
  matching real supply entries and accelerate Energy to the printed destination.
  This is in addition to the regular once-per-turn Zone attachment and does
  not substitute the user's regular once-per-turn Energy from hand.
• If a card says use cards FROM HAND, those cards must exist in hand; Blastoise
  Rain Dance is not authorised to consume Zone Energy on an empty hand.
• If a card says recover cards FROM DISCARD, it must use genuinely discarded
  cards from Basic Energy discard and/or physical discard; never the supply.
• If a card says shuffle recovered or attached Basic Energy INTO DRAW DECK,
  that is physical drawable Energy and can increase the physical deck count.
• Basic Energy attached from hand (even when generated by another effect) and
  Special Energy attached from hand use the same ONE normal hand attachment
  allowance, independently of the ONE normal Zone attachment allowance.
• Card-effect acceleration can attach additional Energy beyond both normal
  allowances. Top-N actual-deck inspections are NOT automatically Zone searches.
• Classic and Pocket Energy engines remain separate.

SHARED LOGIC CHANGES
--------------------
app/src/main/assets/tcgp-battle-core.js
  Distinguishes handEnergyUsed from energyUsed, resets both on turn boundary,
  checks both in legal actions and validates before normal attachments.
app/src/main/assets/tcgp-battle-effects.js
  Adds typed/filtered supply search, recovered Energy to physical deck,
  normal hand-only attachment fidelity, and choice prompts for mixed sources.

LIMITS / QA
-----------
• No Android touchscreen test or installed APK build was performed.
• This is a wide SHARED-ENGINE review plus key card-specific corrections,
  not a claim of exhaustive proof for every card printed across all sets.
• Earlier pre-existing UI and deck/Prize-format CHECK items in the master
  rules are still open; this patch does not change Bench/Prize design.
• Rare effects involving a top-N Energy-zone conversion, custom cards with
  special source wording, and very large mixed-era combinations should be
  audited as encountered. Do not infer the correctness of an untested card.

QUICK MANUAL QA
---------------
1) Have two discarded Basic Energy. Play Neo or EX Deoxys Energy Charge on
   heads, choose both. The draw-deck count rises by two; Energy Zone does not.
2) Attach Basic from normal Zone, then attach a physical Basic (or Special)
   from hand in the same turn. A second ordinary hand attachment is blocked.
3) Use Blastoise Rain Dance without Water in hand: no substitute Zone Energy.
   Put Water into hand using a legal search, then use Rain Dance to accelerate.
4) Play EX Emerald Mr. Stone's Project; choose deck search and select a fresh
   Basic Energy type from Zone. It appears in hand for later use.
5) Confirm that Power Plant can trade a Basic Energy from hand for a different
   type already in its Basic Energy discard ledger.
