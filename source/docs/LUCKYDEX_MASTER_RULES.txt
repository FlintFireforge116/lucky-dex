LUCKYDEX — MASTER PROJECT RULES AND DEVELOPMENT CONTRACT
=========================================================
Document version: 2.20
Compiled: 12 November 2026
Current source baseline inspected: v0.69.226; v0.69.227 expands Diamond & Pearl to 110/130 battle-enabled prints, with Bench-only Unown Powers, accurate attached-card return, counter targeting, Active promotion and opponent-controlled selections. 20 cards remain gated, including all LV.X.
Recommended repository location: ~/lucky-dex/source/docs/LUCKYDEX_MASTER_RULES.txt
Project: LuckyDex — Android application/PWA, managed with Termux and GitHub

PURPOSE
-------
This file is the long-term source of truth for developing LuckyDex across new ChatGPT conversations and many future Pokémon card expansions. Read it BEFORE implementing features, fixing bugs, changing a game rule, choosing card metadata, or packaging a patch.

This is a living contract, not a claim that every rule is already correctly implemented. It records (A) user-approved design requirements, (B) behaviour observed in the inspected source, (C) known exceptions/bugs, and (D) proposals needing approval. If these conflict, DO NOT quietly assume the existing code wins. Flag the difference, verify the previous decision and printed card text, and ask the user only when a new design choice really is required.

Use these labels consistently:
  [LOCKED]    Explicitly agreed or repeatedly reinforced. Do not change without permission.
  [CURRENT]   Observed in the inspected code; not automatically an approved design decision.
  [EXCEPTION] Documented LuckyDex conversion from official rules; preserve its scope.
  [CHECK]     Historical disagreement, unverified interpretation or unresolved issue.
  [PROPOSED] New recommendation; not agreed until user accepts it.
  [BUG]       Behaviour known to be wrong; do not memorialise as a rule.

RULE PRECEDENCE AND CONFLICT MANAGEMENT
---------------------------------------
1. User's latest explicit instruction or approval has highest design authority.
2. An explicitly documented, previously approved LuckyDex adaptation takes priority over a generic assumption about official TCG mechanics.
3. For individual cards, authoritative printed text and official errata determine the card's own effect unless a specific LuckyDex conversion is approved.
4. This file defines shared implementation, UI and testing expectations. The code is the implementation to audit, not the final authority on correctness.
5. A test confirms intended behaviour only if its expectation agrees with the rules. A passing test based on a wrong assumption is still a bug.
6. Never infer that an undocumented feature is authorised merely because a similar card or older set did something that way.
7. When two instructions conflict: preserve existing user experience, document the contradiction as [CHECK], avoid unrelated changes, and seek an explicit decision before altering the game rule.
8. Date or version every revised rule. Keep a change history; never silently rewrite a historical decision or disguise a bug as a design adaptation.

PART 2 — GAME-WIDE RULES AND FEATURES
=======================================================================

2.1 Identity and scope
  [LOCKED] LuckyDex is one integrated Pokémon-themed app with collection, packs, trades, Adventure, Fishing, Arcade/minigames, TCG Pocket battles and Classic TCG battles, plus developer tools.
  [LOCKED] Preserve recognizable Pokémon mechanics, card names, types, moves, conditions, art/rarity identities and region context. Custom sets and homebrew rules must be labelled as such.
  [LOCKED] Do not change existing game economies casually. Fishing has a separate currency from general Lucky Coins; card battles should use deliberately balanced rewards, not an uncontrolled farm.
  [LOCKED] Developer testing, forced rare events, test decks and sandbox matches must not grant ordinary rewards, card ownership or achievements unless a specific testing mechanism is explicitly designed to do so.
  [LOCKED] Normal purchases, unlocks, achievements and stats should persist across updates. Save compatibility is a release requirement.

2.2 Usability / mobile-first
  [LOCKED] Android portrait layout is the primary touch target. Avoid PC-only UI patterns, excessive scrolling, tiny hit targets and off-screen controls.
  [LOCKED] Avoid overlays that cover cards, text, active Energy, the Bench or primary actions. Reduce dead space; enlarge relevant information rather than decorating it.
  [LOCKED] Card art must not be stretched or cropped so that cards become unreadable. Portrait cards and landscape Stadiums retain correct proportions.
  [LOCKED] The result of an action must be visible: coin flips, energy attachments/discards, damage, status, CPU actions, rewards, errors and why an action is illegal.
  [LOCKED] Never display the same ambiguous choice repeatedly when the options have different consequences. Explain the difference at the point of choice.
  [LOCKED] Prefer touching/dragging actual cards and board slots over text-only menus; still allow a clear accessible fallback for non-board choices.
  [LOCKED] Do not leave meaningless technical banners or placeholder descriptions on the player-facing battle screen.

2.3 Current subsystems to protect
  Adventure: area progression, Kanto/other region journeys, area art, encounters and rewards; prevent softlocks after League completion. Future expansion should add area-specific interactions rather than only more screens.
  Fishing: rod/bait/shop progression, timed catch mechanic, per-location pools/silhouettes, junk/treasure/rare prizes, and separate currency. Difficulty should depend on rod and execution. Rare drops should have developer test toggles. Keep reward balance (particularly low-value Stardust) under review.
  Arcade: Eager Eevee, Barrier Ball, Pikachu Beach, Streaming Stampede, Tile/Unown puzzles and other established modes. Keep readability/accessibility aids such as Unown magnification where designed. Track scores, milestones and achievements.
  Pack opening: God Packs, rarity reveal and artwork fallback must coexist with collection data. Don't award the wrong rarity or lose the card image/identity.
  Trades: keep the complete offering visible; explain duplicate-species quantities honestly rather than inflating the number of unique offerings.
  Developer settings: allow controlled inspection of rare mechanics without mutating genuine progress.

  These are standing protection requirements, not a claim that every prior backlog item is finished.

PART 3 — CLASSIC POKÉMON TCG FORMAT
=======================================================================

3.1 Core format and rules authority
  [LOCKED] Classic TCG is a CONVERTED format, not a copy of Pocket and not unmodified tabletop TCG. Use original printed card effects while obeying agreed LuckyDex conversion rules.
  [LOCKED] Standard Classic battle decks contain 30 cards at construction. Basic Energy supplied through the Energy Zone is not included in those starting 30 cards.
  [LOCKED] Default copy limit is two copies of the same printed name per deck (unless a stricter printed/card-specific restriction applies). Count same-name print variants together where required.
  [LOCKED] Evolution requirements and Basic/Stage 1/Stage 2 legality remain real, including compatible prior stages, evolution timing and relevant exceptions (such as Rare Candy where available).
  [LOCKED] Normal decks use actual owned cards. Test Lab is an explicit exception, not a way to grant real ownership.
  [LOCKED] Respect printed HP, attack damage, costs, weaknesses/resistances, retreat, Powers, Bodies, Trainer effects, Special Energy and Pokémon identity. Don't rebalance a card numerically without approval.
  [LOCKED] Preserve legitimate prize exceptions: Pokémon-ex and other cards may yield multiple prizes when their rules say so; effects like Shedinja Empty Shell must override the normal award.
  [LOCKED] Classic uses genuine opening/mulligan behaviour and legal opening Basics. Prevent illegal starts, hidden replacements and false deck exhaustion.
  [CURRENT] In v0.69.185 the core config uses 30-card decks, 7 opening cards, 3 Bench slots, 5 physical Prize cards, and a five-Prize win condition.
  [CHECK] An earlier user-approved design specified FIVE Bench spaces and FOUR points to win with different 1/2/3 prize values. THIS CONFLICTS WITH CURRENT CODE AND SCREENSHOTS (3 Bench, 5 prizes). Do not treat the current numbers as permanently approved. Do not silently change them either. Resolve explicitly when revisiting overall Classic format rules, then update this file and tests.
  [EXCEPTION] Classic EX-era references to Double Battle / two Defending Pokémon require a documented single-Active adaptation where LuckyDex cannot model two opposing Actives. Do not globally reinterpret multi-target rules; document each converted card.

3.2 Energy Zone — the most important custom rules
  [LOCKED] Basic Energy normally comes from the SEPARATE Energy Zone / ordered Energy supply, not from the 30-card starting deck.
  [LOCKED] An Energy Zone Basic Energy attachment is independent from the standard once-per-turn attachment of an Energy card from the HAND. The two allowances must not be conflated. Card effects can attach additional Energy as printed.
  [LOCKED] If the deck generates more than one chosen type, Energy generation is random but the queue order is real. The next Energy shown must be the next taken: WHAT YOU SEE IS WHAT YOU GET. No silent fishing for a preferred type or reshuffling the next preview behind the player's back.
  [LOCKED] The normal supply of Energy is effectively unlimited; the visible queue controls the in-battle order, not finite deck ownership. For card text referring to a deck, discard or hand, use the appropriate explicit destination as below.
  [LOCKED] Darkness and Metal require correct distinction: Basic types may come from the Energy Zone when enabled; old Neo/EX-era Special Darkness and Special Metal are real physical Special Energy cards with their printed special effects.
  [LOCKED] Special Energy is part of the physical 30-card deck and obeys printed attachment, effect, discard, deck-search and return rules.
  [LOCKED] Energy attached to Pokémon is represented as actual attached units, with types, source, restrictions and, for Special Energy, card identity/effect information.
  [LOCKED] A generated Basic Energy that an effect puts into the HAND is a temporary, real hand card for relevant hand-based costs, reveals, discards and hand size. Attaching it from hand obeys the normal hand-attachment allowance unless a printed effect grants an extra attachment.
  [LOCKED] An effect that explicitly searches a player's Energy Supply/Zone must take from the correct supply and preserve queue semantics as required by that effect. An effect that searches DISCARDED Energy must not search the supply instead.
  [LOCKED] When Energy is discarded from an attached Pokémon or by a card cost, Basic Energy goes into the owner's separate Basic ENERGY DISCARD, and physical Special Energy goes into the owner's normal card discard (unless printed card text or a properly documented exception says otherwise).
  [LOCKED] A Knocked Out Pokémon's attached Basic Energy also goes into the separate Basic Energy discard. This is essential for later Energy recovery and must not vanish into the infinite pool.
  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-015: A generic deck search for ANY card (for example Base Set Computer Search) must search ONLY the actual draw deck. It must NOT generate Basic Energy from the Zone. The Zone substitution is permitted ONLY for an explicit search for Basic Energy, respecting eligible types and card-specific restrictions; it never makes an absent physical card appear.
  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-016 (v0.69.210): A Power/attack that looks at the top N cards of the physical draw deck (for example EX Crystal Guardians Charizard δ Peal of Thunder) reveals ONLY those real cards. It cannot substitute or silently sample the separate Basic Energy Zone. A builder focused on such a Power should prefer compatible, owned physical Special Energy when available and eligible to attach, *before* generic Trainer filler. Where no compatible owned physical Energy is present, keep the printed limitation and warn rather than inventing Energy.
  [LOCKED] C-DECK-EVO-007 (v0.69.210): When a named evolution has multiple owned prints of a pre-evolution, favour the variant with useful attacks payable by the final attacker's Energy plan. Never silently turn optional off-type attack requirements into extra Basic Energy types when a compatible variant exists.
  [LOCKED] C-EX14-CHARIZARD-008 (v0.69.210): Double Rainbow supplies two flexible units only on evolved non-ex Pokémon and reduces outgoing attack damage by 10; Peal of Thunder respects its attach restrictions. Charizard δ Metal Burn discards actual attached Energy currently providing Metal (Basic Energy to the basic ledger; physical Special Energy to card discard). Crystal Beach can change which types Special Energy currently provides.
  [LOCKED] A card that retrieves discarded Energy searches ALL eligible discard destinations, not just the visible ordinary discard pile or just the Basic Energy counts.
  [LOCKED] Recovered Energy MUST arrive at the EXACT destination specified: (1) HAND, (2) ACTIVE/BENCH POKÉMON, (3) DRAW DECK, (4) ENERGY ZONE/SUPPLY, or (5) another named zone. These are not interchangeable operations.
  [LOCKED] If a card says 'choose' Energy, the player selects eligible cards/types/targets and count. Do not silently use the first, newest, most convenient or cheapest candidate.
  [LOCKED] If a card explicitly shuffles Energy into the draw DECK, that is a real draw-deck operation even when the Energy was originally Basic Energy generated by the Zone. It does NOT merely refill the Energy Zone.

If Basic Energy is in the dedicated ledger, a recovery effect choosing discard must find it there; return it to HAND if that is the printed destination. Preserve which source was chosen.

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-001 | Four zones are DISTINCT: ordered fresh Basic Energy

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-003 | Ordered-supply requirements are strict: the normal

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-004 | Anything saying 'from your discard pile', 'recover

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-005 | Anything saying 'from your HAND' (e.g. Base Blastoise

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-006 | TWO INDEPENDENT NORMAL QUOTAS per eligible turn:
    (A) one Basic Energy placed by normal Energy Zone action, and (B) one
    Energy CARD attached from hand, Basic or Special. Attaching Basic Energy

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-007 | Destination fidelity is non-negotiable. 'Shuffle

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-008 | Exact 'look at the top N cards of your deck',

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-009 | Special Energy always remains physical: no generic

  [LOCKED] C-ENERGY-010 | The CPU and player use identical zone rules, source

3.3 EX Deoxys Energy Charge — permanent regression example
  [LOCKED] Energy Charge (EX Deoxys ex8-86) flips a coin; on heads, choose up to two eligible Energy cards from discard (or fewer if unavailable) and shuffle them into the user's actual DRAW DECK.
  [LOCKED] Basic Energy eligible for recovery must be taken from the Basic Energy discard; Special Energy from the regular discard. If two are recovered, the DRAW DECK count increases by two. The Energy Zone count does not increase because of this effect.
  [LOCKED] The recovered Basic Energy is represented as a physical card in the draw deck; when drawn it becomes a real hand card and follows ordinary hand attachment/card-play rules, not a free second Energy Zone allowance.
  [LOCKED] On tails no recovery occurs; if only one eligible Energy exists recover one; if none exist don't display a fake choice or create Energy out of nothing.
  [BUG / HISTORICAL] v0.69.183 incorrectly returned Basic Energy to energySupply rather than the draw deck. v0.69.185 corrected that. NEVER reintroduce that previous expectation as a passing test.
  [LOCKED] Regression must check the actual deck size/count, discard decrements, choice identity, later drawability, hand attachment cap, tails and no-eligible cases — not merely that the card action returned success.

3.4 Other Energy card examples and tricky cases
  Camerupt EX Deoxys Energy Draw: select a legally recoverable Energy and the correct receiving Pokémon; preserve printed attachment requirements.
  Metagross EX Deoxys Super Connectivity: choose eligible discarded Psychic/Metal Energy from the applicable discard zone(s), attach to a legal target and apply any printed damage counter only when the recovery happens.
  Energy Flow: where the card returns Energy to hand, it must really appear in hand, not be lost, discarded or added to the supply.
  Hoppip/Special Condition choices: when text gives a choice of conditions, allow selecting the correct condition, not a forced default.
  Moltres and other recovery effects: allow choosing eligible Energy from the right discard zone; don't silently pick a type/target.
  Special Rainbow/Boost/Heal/Scramble Energy and earlier Darkness/Metal: preserve restrictions, effects, number of Energy units provided, duration and expiry. Rainbow should not accidentally count as multiple Energy units when it grants one flexible unit.
  Energy removed by an attack cost must really be discarded, and the AI must pay the same costs. Electabuzz accumulating 10 Energy without using a discard-requiring attack is an example of behaviour to guard against.
  Cards that inspect the 'top Energy' must consume the real displayed queue order; do not choose from the infinite conceptual pool instead.

3.4c CLASSIC MAXIMUM HAND SIZE (introduced v0.69.195)
  [LOCKED] C-HAND-001 | Classic TCG has a HARD maximum of EIGHT physical cards
    in each player's hand, including the CPU. Pocket retains its own ten-card
    cap; do not apply this Classic-only adaptation to Pocket.
  [LOCKED] C-HAND-002 | Ordinary draws and Energy/deck searches cannot exceed
    eight cards. If the hand is already full, leave undrawn cards in their
    source draw deck; never consume a card while reporting no draw.
  [LOCKED] C-HAND-003 | Multi-card forced returns, prize awards and legacy
    effects may enter the hand together. After EACH actual battle action,
    bring both sides down to eight: keep newest returned/awarded cards so
    their intended effect survives, move the oldest excess cards intact to
    the BOTTOM of their owner's draw deck, and log their identities and count.
    Do not silently delete, discard or duplicate excess cards. This is the
    documented LuckyDex hand-cap conversion for otherwise mandatory effects.
  [LOCKED] C-HAND-004 | Previously saved Classic battle states with no hand
    limit migrate to eight when the next action is processed. Test draw cap,
    attacker/defender, forced-return Unown O, CPU and energy-in-hand paths.

3.5 Prize cards, deck and searches
  [LOCKED] Prize cards are a separate hidden zone and must never randomly enter the hand or be treated as ordinary draw deck cards without an explicit card effect.
  [LOCKED] Searching the draw deck cannot retrieve a card currently set aside as a Prize. Deck exhaustion, Prize claim and 'cards drawn' are distinct events.
  [LOCKED] Pokémon-ex/other prize-changing effects and special no-prize KOs override the standard prize process in their specified cases.
  [LOCKED] Preserve legitimate actions that look at or manipulate Prize cards; adapt physical-prize mechanics carefully for the converted 30-card format. Document every conversion; do not fake an arbitrary result.
  [LOCKED] Real deck manipulations (draw, shuffle, recover, look at top N, reorder, choose specific card, send to bottom) must modify the correct ordered zone and allow required choices.
  [LOCKED] Search effects can find only eligible cards present in the zone searched. Avoid 'search succeeded' messages when the card is actually in a Prize zone or absent.
  [LOCKED] The draw deck is initially 30 minus opening/prizes/draws, but may later INCREASE after card effects shuffle recovered cards (including converted Basic Energy) into it. Never hard-cap in-battle draw-deck size at 30.
  [LOCKED] When an item moves a card from draw deck to hand, or vice versa, it must preserve identity and ownership, and deal correctly with generated Basic Energy where applicable.

3.6 Attacks, Powers, Bodies and legal action timing
  [LOCKED] Implement every printed condition and clause, including optional clauses, thresholds, self-damage, extra damage, prevention, attack locks, targeting, evolution restrictions, hand counts and status conditions.
  [LOCKED] An effect cannot be treated as plain damage merely because damage is easy to implement. 'Special effect' placeholders are not an acceptable completed result.
  [LOCKED] Weakness, resistance, retreat modifiers, effects of attached Energy and Stadiums must all apply at the correct stage of resolution.
  [LOCKED] [C-ATTACK-001, 2026-11-10] A printed next-turn attack boost must persist to the designated following turn, modify ONLY the named attack (with its printed duration), and change real damage before Weakness and Resistance. The attack selection UI must show the same boosted base-damage preview; the artwork/printed card text can stay unchanged. Both player and CPU use the same rule.
  [LOCKED] [C-ATTACK-001 examples] Jungle Scyther (base2-10/base2-26) Swords Dance makes next-turn Slash 60 base instead of 30; EX Deoxys Ninjask (ex8-13) Swords Dance makes next-turn Slash 80 base instead of 30; EX Sandstorm Vigoroth (ex2-52) Focus Energy makes next-turn Slash 90 base instead of 40. These bonuses expire if the eligible following turn passes unused.
  [LOCKED] [C-ATTACK-001 coverage] An attack's own printed effect text can be empty yet still depend on a prior effect. Never replace such an attack's runtime rule with a no-op purely because its text field is blank (e.g. ex8-13 Slash depends on Swords Dance). Verify both gameplay damage and visible preview in tests.
  [LOCKED] A printed inability to use ONE attack must not disable another available attack. Example: Scyther's Slashing Strike restriction must not prevent Bounce merely because Bounce is its only other legal attack.
  [LOCKED] When an effect suppresses effects of attacks, that does not necessarily prevent damage; and prevention of extra effects must not become damage immunity (historic Machamp bug).
  [LOCKED] Pokémon Powers, Bodies and Abilities have correct activation conditions, once-per-turn limits, Bench-only/Active-only requirements, status suppression and timing. Do not offer inactive Powers as playable actions.
  [LOCKED] Stadium actions must check player ownership/turn state, conditions and actual benefit. The AI should not spam Stadium replacements or use healing at full HP.
  [LOCKED] Status conditions are individually meaningful (Poison, Burn, Asleep, Paralyzed, Confused), including immunities, removals, conditional damage, checks and actual player choices where printed.
  [LOCKED] Coin flips must happen visibly and use the correct outcomes; moves with several flips must resolve the printed number and result semantics.
  [LOCKED] Tools, Stadiums, Supporters and ordinary Trainers must be distinguished. Playing a Supporter consumes only the Supporter allowance unless a separate effect restricts Trainers.
  [LOCKED] Opponent effects can restrict Trainer use on the FOLLOWING turn when printed; they must expire at the correct time and visibly explain the restriction. Example: Manectric ex Disconnect prevents non-Supporter Trainers for the affected next turn; it does not forbid a permitted Supporter or stay locked indefinitely.
  [LOCKED] If a card is unplayable, show the reason (turn limit, prior Supporter, opponent lock, no valid target, insufficient Energy, etc.) rather than silently swallowing taps.

3.7 Clear and accurate player choices

  [LOCKED] Identify board targets by unique instance/UID/physical Bench slot, not by Pokémon name alone. Two identical species are separate targets.
  [LOCKED] When choosing multiple attributes (which Pokémon, which attached Energy, how many Energy, which effect), present the stages clearly and do not silently choose an arbitrary target.
  [LOCKED] Choosing a discard/deck/hand card should show recognizable card art/names and the relevant source zone, then confirm before irreversible actions when helpful.
  [LOCKED] If an effect offers skip/cancel/optional choice, preserve it. 'Skip' must not unintentionally consume a card or apply damage.
  [LOCKED] C-EX14-POWER-009 (12 Nov 2026): Charizard δ (ex14-4) Peal of Thunder is an OPTIONAL evolution-triggered Power. Offer Use/Decline BEFORE looking at or removing the top five cards.
