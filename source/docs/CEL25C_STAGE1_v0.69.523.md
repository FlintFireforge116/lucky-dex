# Celebrations Classic Collection stage 1 — v0.69.523

The pinned Classic Collection is a separate 25-print catalogue (31 attacks, 13 Powers/Bodies/Abilities, 3 Trainers). All 25 records are imported and 21 are enabled. Main Celebrations remains 25/25. The offline compact source matches the independently retrieved pinned blob's compact transfer FNV-1a32 (226455872); SHA-256 is pinned for local builds. Collector numbers are display metadata, not gameplay keys: four number-15 records have distinct `15_A1`–`15_A4` gameplay suffixes and `15_A`–`15_D` art keys. Ownership projection accepts those keys and explicit canonical identities; ambiguous bare 15 without corroborating metadata is rejected.

- 19 explicitly reviewed historical descriptors were reused. The copied cards keep their own IDs, art, printed rules, costs, historical weakness and resistance amounts, power categories, Baby, LV.X, SP, Star and EX/GX metadata. The original spelling variation of Imposter/Impostor Professor Oak shares the four-copy deck cap.
- Xerneas-EX uses the already supported single-target Bench damage and next-turn named attack lock. M Rayquaza-EX uses own-Bench count damage, existing Delta Evolution and Mega Evolution turn ending; a matching historical Rayquaza-EX can evolve into this print. This is not the non-Mega Rayquaza ex from older EX sets.
- Mew ex Versatile must retain its copy-attack path, exclude other copies of Versatile, and preserve suppression. Team Magma's Groudon shares the original Power Saver count across both fields. Garchomp C LV.X shares its next-turn Dragon Rush lock. Tests use reprint IDs, not the originals in place of them.
- Claydol and Gardevoir ex reprint Powers check all Special Conditions, including Poison and Burn, per printed wording. Tapu Lele-GX Wonder Tag allows decline. Original cards with weaker historical metadata are separate cross-era audit targets.
- Both builders and the set picker register `cel25c`. Classic Collection alone can build legal compatible-type decks (Lightning/Psychic verified), but many single-type combinations lack enough Basics, evolution parents or Trainers; don't silently widen the set pool. Combined Celebrations and Classic Collection is also verified. Include older expansions if you want to use the historical Stage 2 and LV.X lines with their actual parents.

Four remaining gated cards: original Item Here Comes Team Rocket! (persistent face-up Prizes across later swaps/additions), Dark Gyarados (Final Beam KO reaction with typed Energy count, coin, status and matchup), Birthday Pikachu (explicit in-game birthday rule, no guessed personal date), and Umbreon Star (optional hidden hand discard and damage bypass). The current gap ledger is generated by the offline contract.

Android APK compilation, device testing and independent whole-set audit remain outstanding.
