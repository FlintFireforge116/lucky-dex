# LuckyDex v0.69.329 — Whole-Classic attack Energy audit

**Scope:** all existing Classic cards/sets, not just Reshiram or Flashfire. No Codex.

## Confirmed and repaired

- The three Platinum/BW Plasma expansion generators store **508 moves** (BW8=194, BW9=175, BW10=139) with their costs as JSON arrays. The exact count is 508 across these three sets: 194 + 175 + 139 = **508**. A separate `String(cost)` at the CPU, auto builder or attack effect would turn `["R","R","C"]` into `"R,R,C"`. The extra commas become phantom cost positions. `tcg-classic-battle-data.js` now normalizes every registered move to a compact canonical Energy-symbol string before it is consumed anywhere. The original source print records and effect definitions are preserved.
- Existing v0.69.328 fixes are retained: original Reshiram costs, Double Freeze two logged coin tosses with 40 damage per heads and Paralysis on any heads, XY1/XY2 visible era selectors, and Sneasel Flash Claw audit selecting the actual second attack rather than Scratch.
- XY introduces **Fairy Energy** (Y); the shared icon registry previously treated this as ordinary Colorless. Added authentic Fairy attack icon, Fairy Basic Energy print, correct label and Fairy mappings in energy discard/lost-zone utilities.

## Offline verification (uploaded ZIP + v0.69.328 + this patch)

- **6,824 registered Classic cards**; **9,725 attacks** with no malformed printed cost codes after normalization. Covers 59 set IDs at source-ZIP state.
- All 9,725 attacks pass symbolic cost payment, fail with a missing Energy unit, do not let Colorless replace typed, and render exactly the printed icons; inspected each via the actual Classic battle-menu availability code, no exceptions.
- Compared every registered attack in the bundled source-print fixtures for **15** sets (BW1–BW10, HGSS1–HGSS4, Call of Legends); **zero energy-cost mismatches**. This verifies these available print fixtures, not every print against an independent off-device catalogue.
- Double Colorless, Fire-for-Colorless, Fairy-symbol, and special-energy controls included. New test: `tests/v07329-classic-global-energy-audit.test.cjs`.
- Full new audit: **6 / 6 local tests pass**. Previous v0.69.328 fixes: **5 / 6 tests pass locally**, and the sixth explicitly requires XY1 and XY2 generated by Gradle (empty source placeholders are intentional). The corrected Flashfire completion test only runs completely after generation.
