# LuckyDex v0.69.356 — Roaring Skies Stage 4: Sky Field

## Baseline and scope

GitHub Actions #644 successfully built v0.69.355 (Roaring Skies Stage 3). This Stage 4 patch expands battle-ready Roaring Skies support with Sky Field #89, implements its eight-Bench board and removal choices, and adds its associated CPU/deck-quality rules. The authentic 112-print xy6 catalogue and immutable source pin remain unchanged; generation still occurs in the GitHub APK pipeline.

## Sky Field #89: implemented rules

- Both players can use **eight distinct, physical Bench slots (0–7)** while Sky Field is in play, not just eight in an internal list. Hand-Bench placement, card searches, effects and CPU see the eight available slots. A ninth Bench placement is illegal.
- The mobile Classic mat renders all eight Pokémon as selectable and inspectable cards in one row without covering the Active. Pocket layout is unchanged. Drag-and-drop and board-target inspection use the full physical slot number.
- When Sky Field leaves play, including replacement by a Stadium or removal by an attack, **the controller of Sky Field chooses first**. Each player above five chooses which Pokémon to discard until only five remain. The discarded Pokémon's entire evolution stack, attached Tools and Special Energy go to the owner's discard; generated Basic Energy is put in that owner's Energy discard ledger. These are **not Knock Outs**: no Prize cards and no KO triggers.
- If a new Stadium such as Narrow Gym has a stricter cap, resolve the Sky Field discard-to-five first, then the other Stadium's printed or adapted Bench reduction procedure.
- LuckyDex's 30-card Classic adaptation ordinarily starts with 3 Bench slots. To faithfully honour Sky Field's **printed discard-to-five** result, the non-restrictive base cap remains five in a battle after Sky Field has been used, rather than immediately forcing an unprinted second discard to three. A restrictive Stadium still overrides that cap. This intentional adaptation is explicit in the code, tests and master rules.
- When an effect has a pending choice or a Pokémon needs KO promotion, the mandatory Sky Field discard choices are deferred until the existing choice/promotion resolves. No unrelated prompt is overwritten. Opponent-controlled Sky Field also gives the opponent first choice.
- CPU prioritizes preserving evolved, healthy, well-energized or Tooled Pokémon when reducing its Bench; its Stadium evaluator considers actual Emerald Break support and opponent gain instead of generic Stadium spam.
- Autobuilder excludes Sky Field from automatically generated generic filler decks unless an actual Emerald Break Mega Rayquaza line and at least five Basic Pokémon are included. Manual deck choices remain supported.

No APK build success is claimed for Stage 4 until GitHub verifies it.
