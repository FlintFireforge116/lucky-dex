# LuckyDex XY10 Fates Collide — v0.69.383

**Source:** `PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data` pinned `cards/en/xy10.json`, blob `aa097c8da85e960a5afa4f3b96312f64dcbec871`.

## Scope
- 129 official prints (1–125 and 43a, 54a, 105a, 111a), 147 printed attacks, 21 printed Abilities, five BREAK Pokémon.
- Entire official print catalogue is generated by the GitHub APK build. Do not confuse its tracked source-only JS stub with a real generated catalogue.
- First pass projects approximately 73 playable prints, exact number must be read from `docs/XY10_BUILD_COVERAGE.json` after generation.
- Remaining cards require real battle-state choice / trigger mechanics. No unsupported print is exposed to player or CPU as fully playable.

## Reused mechanics
BREAK inherits from its named predecessor using the XY8 audit path. Psychic damage counter scaling, all-board Energy counting, conditional Special Energy boosts, Fairy Retreat zeroing on both boards, two-coin mill, mixed-Bench name bonuses, evolution-only attack shielding, Weakness guard, both-player N redraw by remaining Prizes, Mega Spirit Link turn exemption and DCE physical card energy have coverage in the new tests.

## Deferred groups
- Restored Fossils and bottom-deck fossil search placement with exact evolution and setup semantics.
- Activated/triggered Abilities requiring targeted attachment, hand/deck choices or hand suppression.
- Optional discard/redirect effects with mixed Basic/Special Energy and card identity preservation.
- Team/boardwide passive shields and attacks with delayed KO or conditional extra Prize grants.
- Two-sided Chaos Tower, Strong Energy stacking/off-type handling, Power Memory and alternate multi-hit targeting.
