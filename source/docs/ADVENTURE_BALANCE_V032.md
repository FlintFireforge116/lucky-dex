# Regional Adventure balance and persistence — v0.32.0

Owned Pokémon keep their existing keys, counts, levels, remaining EXP and collection metadata. Missing levels default to 5; valid legacy levels remain (cap 50). EXP required for the next level remains `80 + 30 × current level`. New move fields are additive: four selected move IDs, pending learning choices and learned TM IDs. The existing `luckyDexSave` key and saveVersion 7 are unchanged; regional state has its own schema 1. Existing `adventureV2` runs, collections, currencies, Dash and card systems are retained.

## Campaign levels
- A run snapshots up to three selected partners. Initial battle level is `min(owned level, 8)`; permanent levels remain unchanged. Anchor is the party’s mean starting battle level, clamped to 5–8.
- Each chapter raises the regional level cap by four: `min(50, 8 + chapterIndex × 4)`. Both battle gains and recruited catches respect this cap. Excess regional EXP is bounded below one future level threshold, avoiding a large banked jump. Owned EXP is granted independently and persists.
- Wild/trainer levels: anchor + chapterIndex × 2; rare +1; Gym +2; Elite/finale +3; Champion +4; final clamp 2–48. Current campaigns have nine route chapters plus League finale.
- Opponent move power budget: wild 40 + chapterIndex × 5; bosses 50 + chapterIndex × 5; ceiling 95. Moves come from actual learnsets, not invented names/powers. Evolution-level powerful moves are gated by chapter. Status moves provide role variety. Bosses keep canonical team sizes, including repeated species.
- Battle stats preserve LuckyDex’s formula: HP = baseHP × .55 + level × 4 + 18; ATK = baseAttack × .28 + level × 2.1 + 8; DEF = baseDefense × .28 + level × 2 + 8; SPD = baseSpeed × .22 + level × 1.6 + 6 (rounded). The legacy compact stat model uses ATK/DEF for both damage categories; special/physical remain recognised move roles.

## Moves and accessible combat
- Canonical move type, power, accuracy and PP are bundled. Damage is adapted to the existing reducer; variable/fixed/counter powers use a documented 40-power equivalent, and damaging power is capped at 95 for LuckyDex combat. Display retains canonical power rather than falsely changing source data.
- Type multipliers, accuracy, PP, small 1.1× STAB and level-driven stats matter. Regional incoming damage has a fixed .60 accessibility factor. It applies to all regional foes, not as dynamic rescue or rubber-banding; legacy combat coefficients are unchanged.
- Heal/drain moves restore HP; buffs/debuffs adjust a bounded stance (defence/attack). Burn/poison deal 4% residual damage symmetrically; paralysis has an 18% skipped action chance; sleep/freeze use a two-action drowsy adaptation. Status immunities are respected. Boss AI uses effective attacks plus a support move and at most one heal per team member.
- Splash/Teleport/Transform/Sketch and other non-damaging starter exceptions remain real moves. When no damaging move with PP is available, universal typeless Struggle is available immediately, with an adapted 8% self-HP recoil (minimum 1 HP). PP and status are serialized.
- Native learnsets and 30 curated TMs are offline. Owned and regional four-move sets can differ; replacing an owned move does not silently overwrite a different regional slot. A taught TM may create a regional replacement offer. Permanent high-level learning stays permanent and is gated when used in a capped campaign; legitimately taught TMs are a retained collection advantage.

## Choices, healing and rewards
- Every route chapter offers wild versus a two-Pokémon trainer; camp versus TM versus event/shop; rare encounter versus supply cache; then its Gym/checkpoint. Current campaigns have 77 graph nodes and 41 encounters on a chosen path. Future configuration can use other checkpoint counts, trials, tournament finale arrays and labels instead of assuming eight Gyms/four Elite Four.
- Camp restores 75% HP, all PP, clears status/stance and revives fainted partners. Gym victory restores 70% HP/PP/status and gives a Potion and a typed TM. Victory Road prepares the party fully and adds three Potions. Each Elite/finale restores 50% HP/PP/status and supplies two Potions.
- Regional Potions restore 62% max HP. In battle they cost an opponent turn; outside battle they require a living injured partner and consume one Potion without an enemy action. Legacy Potion healing remains 38%. Shops charge 100 coins for two Potions.
- Defeated foe EXP is summed over the whole trainer team: `40 + foeLevel × 8` per foe, shared with each participating run partner and their owned counterpart. Capture gives `35 + foeLevel × 6` battle EXP; repeat catches retain the existing count and also receive collection EXP.
- Encounter coins: 20 + final foe level × 2; bosses: 100 + chapterIndex × 20. Cache adds 40 + chapterIndex × 5, one Potion and two Poké Balls. Regional clear coins are configured per region. Existing Trainer XP/daily capture/battle/clear hooks and Dex milestones still receive appropriate actual-app events.
- Risk events offer a 65% supply purse (80 + chapterIndex × 5 coins), otherwise 12% party damage. Safe paths recover 12% HP. No event is an unavoidable party wipe.
- Loss/retreat ends only this run. Earned EXP, catches, coins, TMs and supplies persist. Rewards/claims are serialized once, before animation. Reload/background resumes HP, PP, RNG, opponent team position and chosen route.

## Actual balance evidence
Nine deterministic combat simulations used three prepared collected evolved partners (Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise) at level 5, five starting Potions, normal branch supplies and earned TMs. All nine reached eight Gyms and four Elite Four. Eight completed the Champion; the remaining seed lost to the Champion’s final Blastoise after using its finite supplies. Completed parties reached levels 26–28 over roughly 212–283 turns. This is a prepared-team balance regression, not a claim that a single untouched starter wins every run; fresh parties are expected to capture/train and use type coverage. See `artifacts/v0320/regional-balance.json`.

No evolution simulator, held items, abilities, breeding, double battles or every obscure official move mechanic is claimed. Canonical trainer teams are compressed into sequential singles. Other six regions have no placeholder content; see the regional source document and registry.
