## Implementation groups (A–E)

A. Reuse existing descriptors: straightforward attack damage, status, healing, Energy discards, switches, coin flips, draw/search, hand redrawing, prevention and retreat locks.

B. Compose known effects: Typhlosion's top-five Energy count AND real deck discard; Noctowl's two-hand Item count AND reveal; Golurk-style top-deck attacks, Gengar status+bench counters, multiple discard/switch interactions.

C. Parameterise known effects: Raikou/Mewtwo attacks use **Energy units of a specified type**, not the number of distinct Energy types; retreat-cost bonus, hand-count damage, XP/EX targets, typed Energy retrieval, chosen vs random effects, conditional attack bonuses, passive team attack buffs.

D. New shared mechanics still required, and thus currently GATED: Pokémon BREAK inherited cards/attacks/Abilities/defenses; evolution-triggered abilities; Stadium orientation/side effects for Parallel City; all-you-like Energy attach from hand/zone; meaningful physical Special Energy replacement and triggered returns; conditional tool effects; dynamically chosen attack-cost reduction; special attack timing.

E. Card-specific exceptions still required where generic descriptors cannot reproduce printed text: e.g. Damage Change, Dragon Dance, opponent-discard selections, and interactions with exact discard ordering, top-card matching, or damage thresholds.

No fake noops: attack names with nonempty unresolved printed text receive an `unsupported` descriptor; their print has `battleEnabled=false, deckEligible=false` and human-readable `support.reasons`. An unimplemented Ability/Trainer/Tool/Stage BREAK also gates its whole print. Pure blank printed attack text can correctly be noop.

## Examples of deliberately NOT misrepresented effects

- Pinsir's Overhead Throw must damage precisely one chosen *own* Benched Pokémon, not every own Benched Pokémon.
- Mega Houndoom's Inferno Fang must discard optional Fire Energy **before** adding attack damage, not as a late asynchronous choice.
- Mega Glalie's Cryo Mouth requires exactly ten or more damage counters, not merely any damage.
- Raichu's Thunderclap Shot must cover all opponent EX, active AND Bench.
- Mega Mewtwo's Vanishing Strike only bypasses damage effects/Resistance with a Stadium in play, not always.
- Xerneas's Rainbow Force counts *different types on Bench*, not number of Pokémon.
- Reserved Ticket puts a chosen card on top of the shuffled deck, **not into the hand**.
- Burning Energy and Rainbow Energy need actual Special Energy semantics; do not approximate them with standard Energy Zone units.
- All six BREAK evolutions are kept gated pending inheritance rules and tests.
