LuckyDex v0.69.100 — Neo Discovery completion

- All 75 Neo Discovery numbered card prints are registered and battle-enabled.
- Added the remaining 38 cards' rule handlers, including Forretress Spikes,
  Wobbuffet Counter, Politoed Frog Song, Smeargle Sketch, Eevee/Unown powers,
  persistent attack effects and all four Neo Discovery Trainers.
- Darkness and Metal Energy remain real Neo Genesis Special Energy cards;
  the standard Basic Energy Zone still generates six standard types.
- Fossil Egg can bench a qualifying Fossil Evolution from deck or hand.
- For compatibility with the fixed 3-slot Classic Bench, Unown [F] and
  Unown [U] count their four named Unown across Active + Bench. This gameplay
  adaptation is explained alongside their printed powers in the card inspector.
- Belly Drum and Energy Ark generate standard Basic Energy through the Zone
  (not through Basic Energy cards inserted into the 30-card deck).
- Competitive auto-build respects the explicitly selected printed Pokemon.
- Confirmed 40 completed simulated Expert vs Expert battles, with 1,802 AI actions.
- Added v07000-neo-discovery-completion.test.cjs with card-specific mechanics tests.
- New release identity: versionName 0.69.100, versionCode 7080.
